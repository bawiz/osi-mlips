Ammonia: Uncertainty Quantification Benchmark

Empirical evaluation of BLIP (Bayesian PaiNN), Deep Ensembles (3 members), and PaiNN + Gaussian Mixture Models on out-of-distribution molecular geometries across the ammonia pyramidal inversion barrier.

What DFT Researchers Need to Know

The Core Problem: MLIPs trained on low-energy, near-equilibrium configs (78 geometries, 0–4.84 kcal/mol) generate confident but erroneous forces when encountering high-energy OOD geometries (129 test structures up to 70 kcal/mol). Without UQ, deterministic potentials silently produce unphysical trajectories.

Why UQ Matters: Calibrated UQ outputs an actionable variance σ² that signals when the model is extrapolating into regions requiring active learning or DFT re-calculation.

How Each Method Estimates Uncertainty

  • BLIP (Bayesian PaiNN): Input-dependent stochastic noise in message/update functions via variational inference (ELBO). 100 MC forward passes for variance + 1 MAP pass for predictions.
  • Deep Ensemble (3 members): Empirical variance across independently initialized PaiNN models (seeds 1, 2, 3).
  • PaiNN + GMM (Single-Model): Negative log-likelihood density of latent representations under 2-component Gaussian mixture.
  • PaiNN Deterministic: Standard point predictor — baseline accuracy, no uncertainty quantification.

Limitations & Operational Boundaries

Reduced Epoch Budget: Local models trained for 6 epochs on laptop CPU (vs 500–1000 epochs in paper). Energy MAE is ~29 kcal/mol vs ~11.5 kcal/mol at convergence. Crucially, uncertainty ranking (ρ > 0.5) emerges rapidly even before force accuracy converges.
Seed Coverage: Local runs executed with 1 seed (3 seeds for ensemble) vs 4 seeds in published literature. Standard deviations reflect published literature figures.
Single Small Molecule: Ammonia (NH3, 4 atoms) along pyramidal inversion. Conclusions on molecular gas-phase barrier extrapolation do not automatically translate to periodic bulk crystals or heterogeneous interfaces.
Architecture Scope: Evaluated on PaiNN message-passing architecture. BLIP explicitly excludes MACE and UMA architectures per Coscia et al. (2025).
Non-Transferability: Calibrated ECE scores on ammonia do not guarantee calibrated uncertainty on other chemical systems without re-tuning.
No Universal Winner: Per Tan et al. (2023), no single UQ method dominates across all metrics. BLIP achieves superior calibration (ECE 0.169), while GMM achieves highest rank correlation (ρ = 0.593) at virtually zero inference overhead.
Not Run

Silica Solid-State Benchmark (SiO2 Glass Melt-Quench)

Evaluating BLIP on 192-atom periodic silica configurations across 300K–3000K melt-quench MD trajectories requires multi-GPU distributed clusters (~48 A100 GPU-hours). This benchmark was not run locally as it exceeds laptop-scale compute budgets.
