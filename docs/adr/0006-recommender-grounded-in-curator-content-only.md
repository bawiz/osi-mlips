# Recommender reasons only over curator-authored content, serialized fresh into every call

**Status:** historical for the original main-branch chat. ADR-0011 replaces the
mounted chat with structured evidence discovery. It does not feed community text
to this prompt or automatically promote contributions into curated claims.

The Recommender calls Claude through the `sample` capability (memoryless — the page sends instructions and data fresh on every call). We decided it serializes the full curator-authored corpus — Models' Descriptive Lens fields, Known Failure Modes with their literature Evidence and grade, Known Variants, Lineage, Fixes, and the Bayesian Lens's precomputed OOD/decomposition data — into the prompt on every call, instructs Claude to answer only from that data and name a gap explicitly rather than fill it from general knowledge, and deliberately excludes live Community Annotations (community Evidence, Mechanistic Findings, Bayesian Annotations) from that context entirely.

**Why:** the Hub's whole premise is curated, evidence-graded claims — "no number without a source" is already the rule for benchmark figures in the Descriptive Lens. A Recommender that free-reasons from Claude's general knowledge of MACE/SevenNet would break that: its answers would stop being traceable to anything the Hub actually vetted, and could drift from or outright contradict the Hub's own curated claims about the same Model. Excluding Community Annotations closes a prompt-injection path, not just a quality one: that content is posted live with no review queue from unverified contributors (see Contributor, Community Annotation), so folding it verbatim into every Recommender prompt would let anyone's "finding" text attempt to steer the Recommender's behavior for every future viewer, not just report a claim about one Model. Serializing the whole corpus per call (rather than giving Claude tool-calling functions to look up data on demand) was chosen because the corpus is small enough to send whole, and matches the Hub's existing bias toward precompute-and-serve over live lookup machinery (ADR-0001, ADR-0004).

**Considered options:** unconstrained reasoning from Claude's general MLIP knowledge, Hub data as helpful context only — rejected, breaks traceability to curated claims. Hybrid, with general-knowledge fallback clearly flagged — rejected as added complexity not worth it while the curated corpus is already substantive. Including Community Annotations labeled as untrusted in the prompt — rejected for the injection surface, and because the Recommender's authority shouldn't outrun what's actually curated. Tool-calling instead of full serialization — rejected for now given the dataset's current size; revisit if the catalogue grows past what fits comfortably in one prompt.
